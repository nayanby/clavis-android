# Offline narrator

Clavis + exposes the original 1:06 narrator (expr-voice-2-m, speaker 0; preserved under its original-voice label) and Hugo (male, expr-voice-4-m, speaker 4), using Kitten Micro English 0.8. The app runs the model locally through sherpa-onnx 1.13.8. No document or article text is transmitted. Powered by KittenML.

The archive is SHA-256 verified and safely extracted into private storage. Retired Kokoro and Piper/Joe model data is removed after successful installation. Runtime/model Apache licenses and the phonemizer GPL copy are bundled.

The model's full silence durations are preserved; connected sentences are synthesized together. Rate changes are generated by the model. PCM passage caching prepares the next chapter ahead. Ebook narration uses semantic EPUB chapters without figures/captions, and the original MuPDF reader remains on-screen with an inline transport. Native text that is being spoken stays at full opacity; other text is faded to 50% without modifying page images or drawing colored highlights.
